# UGUCOIN Roadmap

## Our Direction

UGUCOIN is being developed progressively.

Our long-term direction is:

**Community → Contribution → Utility → Ecosystem**

The roadmap represents our current development direction. Milestones may evolve as the project grows and community needs become clearer.

---

# What Success Means

UGUCOIN will not measure success solely by token price or market capitalization.

Long-term success means building:

- A genuine community
- Active contributors
- Useful products and tools
- Sustainable utility
- Transparent development
- A recognizable ecosystem

**The goal is to build something people want to participate in—not simply something people speculate on.**
