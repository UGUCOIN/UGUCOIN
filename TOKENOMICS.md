## Supply

The final total supply of UGUCOIN will be determined before launch and publicly documented.

The supply will be fixed according to the final token structure.

---

## Allocation

The final allocation will be publicly documented before launch.

Potential allocation categories include:

- Community
- Community Rewards
- Liquidity / Market
- Ecosystem Development
- Treasury
- Team / Contributors

Exact percentages and amounts will be published before the token launch.

---

## Community Rewards

A predetermined portion of the total supply may be allocated to community rewards.

Rewards may recognize meaningful contributions such as:

- Content creation
- Education
- Community support
- Ideas
- Campaigns
- Development
- Ecosystem contributions

Rewards will not be funded through unlimited token creation.

---

## Team & Contributors

Any allocation reserved for founders or contributors will be publicly disclosed before launch.

The project intends to prioritize transparency around contributor allocations and any applicable vesting or lock-up arrangements.

---

## Liquidity & Launch

UGUCOIN is planned to launch on Solana through Pump.fun.

The initial market formation will use Pump.fun's bonding curve mechanism.

If the token reaches the applicable graduation conditions, liquidity may migrate to PumpSwap according to Pump.fun's protocol.

---

## Token Tax

UGUCOIN is intended to have:

**0% token transfer tax**

UGUCOIN itself will not impose a buy or sell tax.

Platform or network fees may still apply independently.

---

## Supply Policy

The final supply policy will be documented before launch.

Any future changes to token supply, if technically possible, will be subject to publicly documented project decisions.

---
